FamilyOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Overview

FamilyOS is a private, self-hosted household automation application used only by its owner and immediate family. It is not offered to the public and does not sell services or user data.

Google user data accessed

FamilyOS may access Gmail using the Google OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly. This access is used only to read selected email messages and metadata needed for the owner’s personal household automation, such as identifying bills, reminders, deliveries, reservations, or other family-related information.

How Google user data is used

Google user data is processed only for the owner’s personal FamilyOS workflows. The Gmail OAuth integration is read-only and is not used to send, compose, modify, or delete email.

Storage

Derived FamilyOS data may be stored on the owner’s private self-hosted server and in private storage controlled by the owner. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored privately and are not intentionally exposed through this website or shared with third parties.

Sharing and sale

FamilyOS does not sell Google user data. Google user data is not shared with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties. The application is operated solely for private household use.

Data retention and deletion

The owner controls the FamilyOS server and stored data and may delete locally stored FamilyOS data at any time. Google OAuth access may also be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.

Limited Use

FamilyOS’s use of information received from Google APIs is limited to providing the application’s private personal automation functions and is not used for advertising or unrelated purposes.

Contact

For questions regarding this private application, use the support email configured on the FamilyOS Google OAuth consent screen.
