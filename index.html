FamilyOS

FamilyOS is a private, self-hosted household automation system used only by its owner and immediate family. It is not a public service, commercial product, or software offered to third parties.

Purpose

FamilyOS helps organize personal and family information such as calendar events, household tasks, reminders, expenses, meal planning, and selected email-derived facts needed for personal automation.

Google account access

When Gmail access is enabled, FamilyOS uses the Google OAuth scope gmail.readonly solely to read selected messages for the owner’s own personal automation. FamilyOS does not request permission to send, modify, delete, or compose Gmail messages through this OAuth integration.

Availability

The application runs on private infrastructure controlled by its owner. There is no public sign-up, no public user account system, and no third-party access to the service.
